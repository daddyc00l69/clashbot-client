CLASHBOT AI - REMOTE CLIENT INSTRUCTIONS FOR YOUR FRIEND
========================================================================

Welcome to ClashBot AI! You have the full authentic ClashBot AI Pro suite
running directly on your laptop with live cloud attack intelligence.

HOW TO RUN:

1. START YOUR ANDROID EMULATOR:
   - Open BlueStacks, LDPlayer, or MuMu Player.
   - Start Clash of Clans.
   - Ensure Android Debug Bridge (ADB) is enabled in emulator settings:
     * BlueStacks: Settings -> Advanced -> Android Debug Bridge -> ON
     * LDPlayer  : Settings -> Other Settings -> ADB Debug -> Local / Remote
     * MuMu      : Settings -> Basic -> ADB -> ON

2. START THE CLASHBOT AI UI:
   - Double-click `run_bot.bat`.
   - The authentic ClashBot AI Pro window will open on your screen!
   - (If needed, verify the server URL in `client_config.json`).

3. CHOOSE SETTINGS & START:
   - In the ClashBot AI interface, configure your desired troops, farming
     thresholds, and options across the tabs.
   - Click "Start Bot"!
   - The bot will automatically automate your emulator while displaying live
     combat logs and loot stats directly in the application window.

4. STOPPING THE BOT:
   - Click "Stop Bot" in the interface anytime.
